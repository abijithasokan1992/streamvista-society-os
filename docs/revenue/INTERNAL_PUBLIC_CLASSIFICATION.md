# Vista OS Revenue Intelligence — Classification Boundary

## 🔒 INTERNAL / PRIVATE
Revenue Discovery intelligence is internal by default.

Includes opportunity scoring, profit estimates, buyer intelligence, commercial-model analysis, unpublished product/service ideas, pricing hypotheses, internal strategy, internal agents/workflows, and founder/business intelligence.

## 🌍 PUBLIC / EXTERNAL
Only intentionally released customer-facing capabilities, products, services, pricing, documentation, APIs, onboarding and authorized account data may be exposed externally.

## Separation Law
INTERNAL → TEST → VERIFY → PRODUCTIZE → APPROVE → PUBLIC

No automatic crossover from internal to public.

## Revenue Discovery Rule
Run discovery across B2B, B2C, D2C, B2B2C, marketplace, SaaS, service, product+service, API/usage, licensing, white-label, commission, freemium, enterprise and hybrid models.

For every capability test single module, tool, app, API/service, product, bundle and enterprise/white-label form.

## Output
Internal result must contain: opportunity, buyer, model, price/range, estimated margin, probability, evidence, readiness, why-now signal and one next action.

Public output must contain only approved customer-facing information. Never expose internal scores, unpublished opportunities, internal margins, buyer intelligence or strategic reasoning unless explicitly approved for external release.

## Status Labels
- INTERNAL-PRIVATE
- INTERNAL-TEST
- INTERNAL-VERIFIED
- PRODUCT-APPROVED
- PUBLIC-RELEASED

Catalogued ≠ Implemented ≠ Tested ≠ Verified ≠ Productized ≠ Public.
